# Storage Cleaner privacy policy

Effective 26 September 2026. Storage Cleaner is provided by Katarina Kralovicova. Contact **michal.kralovic@gmail.com** for support and privacy requests.

## Data used on your device

With your permission, Storage Cleaner reads photo and video metadata and local previews from the iPhone Photos library to group visually similar photos, list screenshots, and show long videos. Full Photos access covers the library; limited access covers only the items you choose. This analysis runs on your iPhone. Storage Cleaner does not upload your photos, videos, metadata, thumbnails, or scan results to us.

If you use the optional Files feature, you choose a folder through Apple's Files picker. Storage Cleaner reads names, sizes, and file contents as needed to find exact duplicate files in that folder. It does not scan other apps' private data. Files analysis runs locally. It does not upload files or their hashes to us.

Selections and scan results remain in the app's memory while it is open. The app does not require an account and has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. It does not request contacts, location, email accounts, or tracking permission.

## Deletion and recovery

Nothing is deleted automatically. If you confirm Photo deletion, iOS moves selected items to Photos' Recently Deleted collection, normally for up to 30 days. If iCloud Photos is enabled, deletion also syncs to other devices using that library. File deletion in the folder you selected may be permanent; recovery depends on the Files provider. The app cannot restore deleted Files items for you. A separate backup is wise for important memories and documents.

## Purchases and messages to support

Apple handles the paid App Store download and payment details. We do not receive your payment card information through Storage Cleaner. If you email us, your email address and message are processed by our email provider so we can reply; avoid sending private media unless needed for a specific support case.

## Your choices

You can deny or limit Photos access and change it later in iPhone Settings. You choose whether to grant Files folder access. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data; it does not remove your Photos library or Files documents. You can contact the address above with a privacy question or request. We will update this policy if the app's data practices change.
