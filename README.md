# Storage Scout support

Storage Scout helps you review visually similar photos, screenshots, long videos, and exact duplicate files inside a Files folder you select.

For help, bug reports, or privacy requests, email **michal.kralovic@gmail.com**. Please do not attach private photos or files unless we specifically ask for a safe sample.

## Common questions

**Why does Storage Scout ask for Photos access?** It needs your permission to find similar photos, screenshots, and videos. Full access scans the whole Photos library; limited access scans only the items you allow. The app does not upload them.

**Does the app delete anything automatically?** No. Even batch suggestions only add items to a review list. You must confirm Photo deletion in the iOS system dialog. Deleted Photos go to Recently Deleted for up to 30 days and, with iCloud Photos, deletion syncs to your other devices.

**Can it clean every app or system cache?** No. iOS protects other apps' data. To review app sizes or offload unused apps, open Settings > General > iPhone Storage.

**Why must I choose a Files folder?** iOS lets third-party apps access only the folder you grant through the system picker. File deletion may be permanent, depending on the provider, so check your backup first.

**How do I change language?** Use iPhone Settings > Apps > Storage Scout > Language. If the phone language is unavailable, the app uses English.
